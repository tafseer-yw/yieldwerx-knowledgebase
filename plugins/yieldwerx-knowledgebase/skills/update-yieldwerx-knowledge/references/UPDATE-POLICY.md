# Knowledge update policy

## Required evidence

Every confirmed change needs:

- a named source;
- its approval state;
- an owner or decision maker;
- a version, edition, or effective date when available;
- the affected module and chapter.

If any of these are unknown, record the item as a question or draft note. Do not
present it as current product behavior.

Do not delete the older source. Keep it in the archive and state what replaced it.

## Chapter rule

Keep each chapter or appendix in a separate Markdown file. Update only the files
affected by the source. Keep the routing index short and point to the relevant files.

## Writing rule

Write for a QA who is new to the module. Prefer:

- `Select the policy and click Run Policy.`

Avoid:

- `Initiate execution of the selected policy orchestration artifact.`

## Review rule

The repository owner reviews changes to current knowledge, source authority, skill
instructions, and distribution files before merge.
